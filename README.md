**📌  Project Overview:**
As part of the Tata Virtual Internship, I analyzed a Retail Dataset of 5,41,909 rows.
I worked on:

🔹**Task 1** – Framing Business Questions
Analysing Retail data from the perspective of business leaders and developed eight key questions to understand what business leaders would want to know from the data.

**Business Questions❓** 

**CEO questions**
1.	What are the monthly revenue trends, and which months show the strongest and weakest performance?
2.	Which countries generate the highest total revenue and order volume outside the United Kingdom, and which markets are most suitable for expansion?
3.	Which products contribute the most revenue and sales volume, and is revenue overly dependent on a small number of products?
4.	What is the financial impact of cancellations and returned items, and are particular products, customers, or countries associated with unusually high cancellation rates?
   
**CMO questions**
1.	Who are the highest-value customers based on total spend, purchase frequency, and average order value, and what characteristics define them?
2.	Which product categories or individual products are purchased most frequently, and which should be prioritised in promotions?
3.	Are there identifiable seasonal purchasing patterns that should shape the timing of marketing campaigns?
4.	Which products are commonly purchased together within the same invoice, and what bundle, cross-sell, or upsell opportunities does this create?

🔹**Task 2** – Data Cleaning, Transformation & Validation
Prepared and validated the data to ensure it was suitable for analysis and visualization.

🔹**Task 3** – Choosing the Right Visuals
Selected appropriate visualizations for different business scenarios and created effective visuals to communicate the data clearly.

🔹**Task 4** – Communicating Insights & Analysis
Analysed the visualizations, identified key insights, and explained through recording, how the findings related to the business scenario.
